# 🚚 ResORoute

## AI-Powered Smart Routing & Risk Intelligence Platform for NER

ResORoute is an intelligent logistics and accessibility platform designed for the North Eastern Region of India.

The system combines route data, weather conditions, traffic, road conditions and incident information to assess transportation risk and recommend safer routes.

## 🎯 Problem

The North Eastern Region faces logistics challenges caused by:

- Difficult terrain
- Heavy rainfall
- Floods
- Landslides
- Road blockages
- Limited connectivity
- Delivery delays

## 💡 Proposed Solution

ResORoute provides:

- Route risk assessment
- Weather intelligence
- Traffic monitoring
- Dynamic route optimization
- Dijkstra-based routing
- Vehicle tracking
- Incident reporting
- Automated alerts
- GIS-ready architecture

## 🧠 Core Workflow

Weather + Traffic + Road Data + Incidents

↓

Risk Assessment

↓

Route Cost Calculation

↓

Dijkstra Optimization

↓

Safest Available Route

↓

Vehicle Tracking + Alerts
